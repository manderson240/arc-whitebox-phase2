## NOT-TRIED, ranked by plausible gain (all small; none explains the leaders' ~40 percent)

Stated: 54 -> 40 block products, nested twice over rank-7 leaves = 48 percent of ordinary leaf products (82.08B -> 49.45B). Plausible gain: cost only, a few percent at best versus our Strassen depth 4-5 (~0.51 of dense); blocked by dimension divisibility (<3,3,6> vs n=1024), arena memory (31 MB headroom) and f32 stability. Needs the PDF for the exact tensor dims; we may not open it, so only the post-text claim is available.
2. **#28 Resummation of the Hermite/Edgeworth readout** (18157 item 1e, 18159 Borel-Pade ladder at 256x32 with "stable fraction 0.297-0.398"). Our closure truncates at fixed order (EDGEWORTH_ORDER_AND_K4I.md). Plausible gain: unknown, likely under 3 percent raw; the Phase 1 attempt was unstable.

4. **#29 Weight-only angular attractor as a free feature** for the final-mean head (18157/14). Speculative; our rich-feature final read corrector died (G-RB74), so expect null. One matvec chain, near-free to screen.
