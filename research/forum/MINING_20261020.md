## 2. Method in brief
Frozen #334316 cumulant estimator (third-order cumulant propagation after Wu et al. 2605.05179; covariance + factorized third-order state, rank cap 7,680) plus a learned terminal residual corrector; "the propagated state is byte-identical to #334316". Components: (i) small coefficient tables fitted on the actual deployed trajectory ("state-matched"); (ii) alternative-basis Strassen (6 levels, Schwartz-Vaknin) and a rank-48 4x4 recursion (arXiv 2609.12027) for cost; (iii) 6,594-parameter per-layer corrector on 33 per-neuron quantities. Nominations: #336611 and #335413.

## 4. "State-matched correction", precisely
From the post: a small table of correction coefficients is fitted "on the actual truncated float32 trajectory", i.e. the state the deployed estimator really carries (not an exact-moment oracle), using "native central-difference responses of the deployed estimator" (finite-difference Jacobian of the final output w.r.t. each coefficient). Objective: "pooled final-layer MSE, not a moment's own oracle." Train/dev/official nets separate. Contrast stated: a correction fitted to a moment's own truth is not the same as one fitted to the final loss through the deployed, truncated chain. Reported gain 3.7978e-9 -> 3.6473e-9. Caveat in the post: "still show a generalization gap".
